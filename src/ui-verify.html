Verify the dice

Every simTrain scenario commits to a secret seed before your first action: its SHA-256 hash is fixed at the start. Each d20 is SHA-256(seed:roll:draw:k) (first 8 hex digits, rejection-sampled, mod 20 + 1). When the scenario ends, the seed is revealed. This page re-derives every roll in your browser (Web Crypto; nothing is sent anywhere except fetching the public export) and checks the server didn't fudge a single die.
